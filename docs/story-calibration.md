# Story calibration and sourcing

The 50 Franco stories are original. No third-party story text is included. Story and question IDs, answer keys, and version 1 remain unchanged, so existing reading progress still maps to the same questions.

## Source review

- [Lingua French reading](https://lingua.com/french/reading/) offers graded A1–B2 material, but no complete C1–C2 sequence. Its [terms](https://lingua.com/terms/) restrict copying, editing, redistribution, and making content publicly available beyond the granted use. We did not import it.
- [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/) has public-domain French literature and explains [reuse conditions](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/permission.html), but does not grade texts by CEFR. Historical prose also introduces language and cultural context unlike Franco's contemporary stories. It could support a separately curated advanced-reading feature later.
- [The French Experiment](https://www.thefrenchexperiment.com/stories) has illustrated children's stories but does not provide an A1–C2 sequence or a reusable license for embedding them here.

## Progression in this collection

| Level | Length in words | Language and reading task |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| A1 | 100–125 | Short, concrete events; familiar everyday nouns and direct chronology. |
| A2 | 125–160 | More connected events, practical explanations, and accessible past-tense narration. |
| B1 | 165–195 | Decisions and consequences, with viewpoints that need to be connected across paragraphs. |
| B2 | 200–235 | Competing constraints, interpretation of evidence, and qualified conclusions. |
| C1 | 230–275 | Institutional context, bias, and implications that are not always stated directly. |
| C2 | 280–335 | Nuanced claims about evidence, representation, and ambiguity, with denser syntax. |

Length and complexity are editorial targets, not a formal CEFR certification. The content test enforces the length bands; a qualified French-language reviewer should validate the vocabulary, syntax, and comprehension questions before these labels are used as an assessment claim.
